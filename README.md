What's this project all about:
I have chosen the Buddha, once again, as inspirational context for this game. The first level is a trial to prove your worth, and the second level is an oppurtunity to cast out the things covered in the 5 beginner vows barring : sexual immorality, killing, drug use, lying, and stealing. Physically, they appeared as a bed, an axe, a cake, a megaphone, and gloves, respectively.

What's the additional skill I worked on?
I did some blender modeling of the buddha, the multi-cubed spawn wall, and the torus and basket for level two.

What was the key challenge or success?
I had a difficult time getting the player to teleport sucessfully to the right location. It seems that there was an offset on the XR player. I'll give you a metaphor for how I solved this: shooting an arrow at a target with a blindfold on (but the target was kinda close to the marksman)
